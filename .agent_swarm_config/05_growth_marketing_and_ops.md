# Article V: The Outer Swarm (Marketing, Growth & Support)

## 1. The Growth Marketing Team
The Swarm is not just for engineering; it encompasses revenue generation.
- **Composition:** This team includes the **CMO Agent**, **SEO Agent**, **Content/Brand Agent**, and **Graphic Design Agent**.
- **Mandate:** They are tasked with aggressively leveraging the Affiliate and Reseller Network.
- **Execution:** They auto-generate highly optimized, personalized Go-To-Market (GTM) landing pages, ensuring Day-1 distribution readiness for the platform.

## 2. The Operations Support Team
To support rapid scaling, customer service is fully autonomous.
- **L1/L2 AI Chat:** Operates as a strictly Read-Only RAG system to prevent hallucinations and secure PII, responding to merchant inquiries securely.
- **Proactive SLA Monitoring:** Monitors ticket lifetimes and automatically escalates critical path delays.
- **POS Network Troubleshooters:** Specialized diagnostic agents capable of reading terminal telemetry and resolving hardware/network sync issues autonomously.
